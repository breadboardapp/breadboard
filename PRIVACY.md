# Privacy Policy for Breadboard

**Last Updated:** 18 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Breadboard ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Breadboard owns the domain names breadboard.moe and fxweibo.com. Web requests to these domains use industry-standard encryption and are routed through [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) and as such, you should consult their privacy policy in addition to ours.

### Introduction

We want to be clear: the Breadboard app is designed with your privacy in mind.

**No Sale of Personal Data:** We do not sell, rent, or trade any of your personal data to third parties.

### Information We Process and Why

The core purpose of the App is to help you retrieve data from external platforms, but we also provide our own services to help facilitate this.
We will split this section into two parts for ease of understanding.

#### Information Shared by The App
- **IP Address and Web Access Logs:**
  - When accessing domains owned by us, access logs containing your IP address, URL accessed, and your User-Agent will be kept for 14 days for abuse and fraud prevention.
  - In some cases, this may include user-created data such as a list of 'tags' in the URL to retrieve a curated feed.

- **Optional API Credentials:** The App allows you to optionally provide API credentials (such as API keys or tokens) for specific external platforms to enable enhanced functionality.
  - **Purpose and Sharing:** These credentials will be stored on your device and will **only** be shared with the specific external platform they are intended for. These credentials will be used solely for the purpose of authenticating your requests to that platform and enabling enhanced functionality on the platform.
  - **User Control:** You are in control of providing these credentials and can revoke them at any time through the App's settings.

#### Information Processed by External Platforms
- **IP Address:**
  - When you use the App to fetch data from an external platform, your device's IP address will be used to make these requests.
- **Other User-created Data:**
  - This may include search queries or aggregated data derived from your App data. Read below to learn more.

You should consult the privacy policy of the specific external platforms you interact with through the App to understand how they handle your IP address and any other data they may collect.

### Following Feed

The App offers the ability to 'Follow' artists.

- **How it Works:** Each artist is represented by a unique tag that is used to retrieve their posts. The user is able to save a list of these tags locally that can be used to build a curated feed of posts.
- **Data Sent:** When requesting or refreshing your Following feed, this list may be sent to a domain owned by us and used to retrieve relevant posts.
- **Purpose:** The sole purpose of sending this list is to retrieve a list of posts by the requested artists using their tags.
- **User Control:** You can choose not to use this functionality by not clicking the 'Follow' button on an artist profile.

When a user has an empty list of followed artists, the App will use posts the user has marked as a 'Favourite' to recommend artists to follow.
This takes place entirely on-device.

If the user chooses to follow one or more artists from the recommendations that the App provides, the resultant list of followed artists may be sent to a domain owned by us.

In some cases, some or all parts of this list may be sent to [Danbooru](https://danbooru.donmai.us/privacy) to retrieve the relevant posts by these artists. Refer to Danbooru's privacy policy to learn how they use your data.

### Post Recommendations

To enhance your experience, the App offers a feature to recommend posts based on content you have previously added to your favourites within the App.

- **How it Works:** When you use this feature, the App sends aggregated metadata derived from your favourited posts as a search query to the relevant external platform. For example, if you have favourited several posts with specific tags or keywords, the App can use these common tags or keywords to find similar content on that platform.
- **Data Sent:** Only the aggregated metadata (like common tags, categories, or keywords) is sent to the external platform as a search query. We do not send your specific list of favourited posts or any personally identifiable information from your device to the external platform for this purpose.
- **Purpose:** The sole purpose of sending this aggregated metadata is to retrieve recommendations for similar posts from the external platform.
- **User Control:** You can choose not to use this functionality by not visiting the "Browse" page within the App. You can set the App's "Start page" setting to something other than "Browse" to prevent this feature from automatically working when you open the App.

### Search History

To improve your user experience and allow you to quickly revisit previous searches, the App stores your search queries locally on your device.

- **How it Works:** When you perform a search within the App, the search terms you use are saved to your device's local storage.
- **User Control and Deletion:** You have full control over your search history. You can delete individual search history entries at any time within the App. You also have the option to disable the search history feature entirely through the App's settings, which will prevent any future search queries from being saved. If you disable search history, any previously saved search history will also be cleared.
- **Data Storage:** Your search history is stored only on your device. It is not transmitted to us or any third party.

### Reverse Image Search

The App provides the ability to perform a 'reverse image search', whereby the user submits an image and the App provides insight such as the artist and original source.
Reverse image search functionality is provided by [SauceNAO](https://saucenao.com/legal.html) and you should consult the SauceNAO privacy policy to learn about how your data and submitted images are handled.

### Artist Sharing

The App provides the ability to share an artist profile with other users. This is in the form of a URL that points to the breadboard.moe domain.
Refer to this document's section about Information Shared by The App to learn more about how your data is used.

### Data Export

The App provides you with the functionality to export your application data to a file. This allows you to back up or transfer your information.

- **What is Exported:** The user has the ability to choose which data categories are exported. The categories available for export include: **App settings**, **Favourite images**, and **Search history**.
- **Sensitive Information Exclusion:** For your security, sensitive information such as API credentials you have saved within the App will **not** be included in the exported file.
- **User Responsibility:** Once you export your data to a file, that file is stored on your device or in a location you choose. The security and management of this exported file become your responsibility. We are not responsible for the exported file once it has been created and saved outside of the App.

### Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect the information we process. When you provide API credentials, they are stored locally on your device, the App's developers do not have access to these credentials, and they are only used for the purpose of making requests to the external platforms you choose to interact with.

As the App does not identify individual users or store data for reasons other than those that are technically necessary, we do not have a process for you to request deletion of personal data. You can, however, delete any stored API credentials through the App, or clear the App's data through your device settings, which will remove all locally stored information.

Web access logs are deleted after 14 days.
